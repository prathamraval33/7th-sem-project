• Current State
Template matching and OCR extraction are complete in fee_receipt_service.py; however, the hard gate in applications.py was temporarily bypassed for testing.

Enforce strict require_fee_verified=True check in POST /applications. Students with unverified receipts will be blocked with an informative 403 modal directing them to upload proof.

Allow TPO officers to grant emergency provisional exemptions for students awaiting bank clearance.
